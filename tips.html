Printer settings that generally work well

Starting points for printing lithophane lamps and their bases. Names match Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer; other slicers use similar names. Every printer is a little different, so treat these as a first try and adjust from your test prints.

The one rule: a lithophane is a picture made of plastic thickness, so the lamp's wall must print solid. Normal settings fill the middle of a wall with a sparse pattern that is mostly air; light scatters off it and the picture looks blotchy and blurry. Set Sparse infill density to 100%.

The lamp or panel (ball, football, Moon, cube, flat panel, lantern panels)

Sparse infill densityStrength tab
100%
Fills the wall solid. The most important setting on this page. (Leave solid infill, which is only the top and bottom layers, as it is.)
Wall loopsStrength tab
3 to 4
Each loop is printed on both the outside and the inside of the shell, so 4 loops cover about 3.4 mm, nearly the whole wall; 100% infill fills the rest. This is what most lithophane guides use.
Layer heightQuality tab or preset
0.12 mm0.08 mm for the finest detail
Much of a lamp is close to vertical, so up-and-down detail can't be finer than one layer. Thinner layers give sharper edges and less banding, but take longer.
Seam positionQuality tab
Back
Each layer starts and ends at the seam, leaving a thin line. Turn the lamp so the picture faces away from it.
Wall generatorQuality tab
Arachne
Handles walls whose thickness keeps changing, like a lithophane. The default in recent versions.
Outer wall speedSpeed tab
About half the default
A cleaner surface and more even light.
SupportsSupport tab
On: tree, build plate onlyOff for the cube, flat panels and every lantern part
Balls, the football and the Moon need them for the curve just above the collar. The cube, flat panels and the lantern are designed to print without them.
BrimOthers tab
On for flat panelsabout 5 mm
A panel stands on a narrow edge; a brim stops it tipping or lifting, especially hearts and tall panels.
Ironing
Off
Not needed.

The base

The base isn't a lithophane, so normal settings are fine: 0.2 mm layers, 2 to 3 wall loops, 15 to 20% sparse infill. Print it in black or another dark, opaque filament so light only comes out through the lamp. If the collar is too tight or too loose, change Collar fit on the Base tab by 0.1 to 0.2 mm and print again.

The lantern's pillars, base and top are the same: normal settings, dark filament. Print the base and one pillar first and push them together: if the peg is too tight or too loose, change Peg fit (Shape tab); if a panel won't slide down a slot, add Slot fit.

Printer and material

Nozzle
0.4 mm
Works well. A 0.2 or 0.25 mm nozzle gives sharper edges but prints much slower. With 0.4 mm, keep the app's Thinnest at 0.8 mm or more (two printed lines).
Filament
White PLA
Use one spool for the whole lamp: brands and even shades of white let different amounts of light through.
Print time
2 to 3× longer
Than a normal print of the same size. That's normal for lithophanes.

Checklist before you print the lamp

  • Sparse infill density 100%
  • Wall loops 3 to 4
  • Layer height 0.12 mm (or 0.08 mm)
  • Seam position Back, picture turned away from it
  • Supports on, build plate only (off for the cube and flat panels; brim on for panels)
  • In the slicer preview, scroll through the layers: the wall should show only loops and solid fill, no open diagonal pattern

If something looks wrong

Blotchy or patchy dark areas
Sparse infill
Sparse infill 100%, wall loops 3 to 4.
Grainy or streaky light
Infill lines show
Try more wall loops (6 to 10) so loops fill more of the wall.
Horizontal stripes
Layer lines
Layer height 0.12 or 0.08 mm; slow the outer wall.
Blurry edges
Layers, glow or contrast
Thinner layers; raise the app's Thinnest to 1.0 to 1.2 mm; raise Contrast on the Photo tab.
Thin vertical line through the picture
The seam
Seam position Back, and turn the lamp.
Very bright spots or small holes
Walls too thin
Keep Thinnest at 0.8 mm or more with a 0.4 mm nozzle.
Whole lamp looks washed out
Light too bright
Raise Thinnest a little, or dim the light.
Light leaks out of the base
Light filament
Print the base in dark, opaque filament.

Tip: to test settings quickly, export a Small lamp with the picture shrunk to fit. It prints much faster and shows the same problems.
